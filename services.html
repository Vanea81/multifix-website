Support

Practical help that keeps units rental-ready

Clear documentation, careful non-structural assistance, and coordination with certified contractors when regulated trades are required.

Licensed trades: Plumbing, electrical, gas, HVAC and structural work are performed by certified contractors only. I can document the issue and coordinate the intervention.

Documentation & reporting

  • Before/after photos
  • Simple summary: what was done, what to watch
  • Priority list for next steps (including licensed trades if needed)

Turnover support (move-in / move-out)

  • Readiness checks and follow-up
  • Small adjustments to improve presentation and comfort
  • Coordination of visits and access (by appointment)

Non-structural assistance

  • Sealing / caulking / minor draft & moisture prevention around trim areas
  • Minor adjustments: doors, handles, cabinet alignment
  • Mounting: TV, shelves, curtain rods, blinds
  • Furniture assembly and small carpentry fixes (non-structural)

Coordination with licensed contractors

  • Collect details (photos, symptoms, location)
  • Help you request quotes / schedule intervention
  • On-site coordination (if needed) and documented follow-up
By appointment: text/email preferred. If you’re unsure whether a task is regulated, send photos — I’ll tell you what is safe to handle now and what needs licensed trades.
