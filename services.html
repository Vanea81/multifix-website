How service calls work

  1. You send the request (form / text / email) + photos if possible.
  2. I confirm schedule. If it’s bigger than a quick fix, I provide an estimate.
  3. I complete the work, keep the area clean, and report back (photos if helpful).

Good fit for

  • Plex & small buildings (2–20 units)
  • Rental turnovers (small fixes between tenants)
  • Preventive maintenance to reduce repeated issues

Not included (separate quote)

  • Major renovations, structural work, full rewiring or re-piping
  • Work requiring specialized licensed trades beyond safe scope

For bigger jobs, I can assess the issue and provide a clear quote (or coordinate with the right specialist).
