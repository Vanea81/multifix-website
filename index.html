Rental-ready support, documentation & coordination

I help owners and small property managers keep units ready for move-in with clear documentation, practical non-structural assistance, and coordination with licensed contractors when regulated work is needed.

Request a visit / estimate See portfolio
Important: Licensed trades (plumbing, electrical, gas, HVAC, structural work) are performed by certified contractors only. I can document the issue and coordinate the intervention.

What I do

  • Photo documentation (before/after) + simple work summary
  • Turnover support: checks, small fixes, readiness follow-up
  • Non-structural assistance: sealing/caulking, minor adjustments, mounting (TV/shelves), assembly
  • Coordination with licensed contractors for regulated work

What I don’t do

  • Plumbing modifications or licensed plumbing work
  • Electrical wiring/panels/new circuits
  • Gas / HVAC projects
  • Structural changes, roofing, facade work

If a task falls into a licensed trade, I document it and connect you with certified pros.

How it works: 1) You send photos + address → 2) I visit by appointment → 3) You get a clear plan: what’s safe to do now, what needs licensed trades.
