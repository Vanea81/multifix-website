About

Calm work. Clear documentation. Real follow-up.

MultiFix Montréal is a small, owner-operated service focused on rental readiness, documentation and coordination. The goal is simple: reduce stress for owners and managers by keeping communication clear and work documented.

Boundaries: Licensed trades (plumbing, electrical, gas, HVAC, structural work) are performed by certified contractors only. I document the issue and coordinate the intervention.

What matters to me

  • Clean work and respect for tenants/common areas
  • Fix what is safe to handle — document what needs licensed pros
  • Before/after photos and simple work summaries
  • Reliable follow-up by appointment

Experience

  • 25+ years of hands-on maintenance and practical problem solving
  • Building upkeep and unit readiness support in Montréal
  • Appliance installation/adjustments only when non-regulated (plug-in / no system modification)

If a task requires a licensed trade, I help you document it and coordinate certified contractors.

Communication style: simple, documented, no chaos. Text/email preferred, visits by appointment.
