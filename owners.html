For owners & managers

One reliable contact for documented follow-up

If you manage a small building or a few units, you need calm, clean, well-documented follow-up — and a clear line between what is safe to handle and what requires licensed trades.

Clear boundaries: Licensed trades (plumbing, electrical, gas, HVAC, structural work) are performed by certified contractors only. I document the issue and coordinate the intervention.

What you get

  • Before/after photos
  • Simple written summary (work log)
  • Priority list: what to do now vs what needs licensed trades
  • Appointments coordinated — no chaos

Typical use cases

  • Move-out / move-in readiness checks
  • Small recurring issues: trim gaps, minor adjustments, mounting, assembly
  • Documenting problems for insurance or contractor quotes
  • Coordinating licensed pros when a regulated trade is required
Workflow:
1) You message photos + address + deadline
2) Visit by appointment
3) You receive a documented plan + clear next steps
4) If needed: I coordinate certified contractors and follow up with documentation

Service style

  • Clean work, calm pace, clear communication
  • Documentation first (so decisions are easy)
  • Respect for tenants and common areas

Appointments & communication

  • Text/email preferred
  • On-site visits by appointment
  • Simple estimate once I see photos or the unit
