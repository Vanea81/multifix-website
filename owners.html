Common pain points

  • Tenant requests every week
  • Evenings lost calling different contractors

Simple solution

One point of contact for ongoing maintenance. I take care of the day-to-day issues and keep you informed.

What I handle (typical requests)

  • Doors, locks, handles, hinges, closers
  • Drywall patches, painting touch-ups
  • Basic plumbing: leaks, traps, faucets, toilets (minor)
  • Basic electrical: outlets/switches/light fixtures (safe scope)
  • Appliance installation/replacement (dishwasher, washer, dryer, etc.)
  • AC / ventilation basic service & installation (when applicable)
  • Preventive checks and small fixes before they become emergencies

How it works

  1. You send a request (form / text / email)
  2. I confirm schedule + estimate if needed
  3. I fix, document, and report back

Service formats

  • On-call service (as needed)
  • Maintenance plan (monthly)

What’s not included (separate quote)

  • Major renovations, structural work, large electrical panels, full plumbing re-pipes
  • Emergency after-hours service (if needed — case-by-case)
  • Jobs requiring specialized licensed trades beyond safe scope

For bigger jobs, I can assess the issue, explain the options, and provide a clear quote (or coordinate with the right licensed specialist).

Quick FAQ

  • Do you work with tenants directly? Yes — with owner/manager approval and clear communication.
  • Do you provide photos/updates? Yes — before/after where helpful.
  • Do you offer preventive maintenance? Yes — to reduce repeated issues and surprises.
